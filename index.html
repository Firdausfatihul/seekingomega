01

Approach

Corporate actions in small IDX companies

We judge our work by one number: the change in net asset value (NAV) per unit, compared with the IHSG, the Jakarta Composite Index.

How we work

  1. Start from the filing

    Corporate actions on the IDX are announced through keterbukaan informasi, the exchange’s public disclosure system. A rights issue prospectus, a tender offer notice or a shareholder meeting agenda is where an idea starts.

  2. Stay with small companies

    We own small listed companies. A single corporate action can change who owns the business and on what terms.

  3. Measure one number

    NAV per unit, with every contribution and withdrawal unitised, set against the IHSG. The periods we did worse stay in the table next to the periods we did better.

  4. Our own capital, in private

    The capital is the partners’ own, pooled. We do not raise money from the public, and we discuss capital only in private conversations.

Situations we look for

Rights issues
HMETD, hak memesan efek terlebih dahulu
Who the standby buyer is, what the proceeds pay for, and what the price does to holders who do not take up their rights.
Tender offers
penawaran tender
The price offered, the conditions attached, and what happens to the shares left behind.
Mergers and acquisitions
penggabungan usaha dan pengambilalihan
The exchange ratio or the price, who ends up in control, and whether a mandatory tender offer follows.
Backdoor listings
pencatatan melalui pintu belakang
A private business enters the exchange through a company that is already listed. We read what is being put in, and at what value.
Restructurings
restrukturisasi
Debt turned into shares, assets sold or a balance sheet rebuilt. We read who owns the company once it is done.
Spin-offs
pemisahan usaha
A business separated from its parent and handed to shareholders who did not choose to own it.
